This free online invoice generator lets freelancers, contractors and small businesses create a clean, professional invoice in minutes and save it as a PDF — with no sign-up, watermarks or limits. Fill in your business details, add line items with quantities and rates, and download a polished document that is ready to send to a client and get you paid faster.

How the Invoice Generator Works

You enter your business and client details, then add line items with a description, quantity and unit price. The tool multiplies quantity by price for each line, adds the line totals together, and applies any tax or discount to produce the final amount due — so the invoice total is always calculated for you automatically.

Because everything runs in your browser, none of your invoice data is uploaded to a server, so your client details and figures stay private on your own device. When you are ready, use the Print button and choose "Save as PDF" to download a copy or email it straight to your client. A complete, professional invoice should include a unique invoice number, the issue and due dates, an itemized list of work with amounts, any applicable tax, and clear payment terms such as the accepted payment methods and how many days the client has to pay.

Example: 10 hours of design work at $75/hour produces a line total of $750; add a second line of $120 and the invoice subtotal becomes $870.
Example: an $870 subtotal with an 8% sales tax adds $69.60, giving a total due of $939.60 on the finished invoice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this invoice generator free?

Yes, completely free. No sign-up, no watermarks, no limits.

Can I save the invoice as PDF?

Yes — use the Print button and select "Save as PDF" in your browser's print dialog.

Is my data stored?

No — everything stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

What should a professional invoice include?

Include your business name and contact details, the client's details, a unique invoice number, issue and due dates, itemized line items with amounts, any tax, and clear payment terms.
